# Prompt examples

Attach the relevant image with any of these prompts. Replace bracketed values with your details.

## Reusable template

Use $social-image-post with the uploaded image.
Platform: [Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn / X / TikTok / Pinterest / other]
Description: [What you want to communicate, including important facts]
Tone: [Friendly / professional / playful / elegant / inspiring / other]
Optional preferences: [Language, audience, CTA, maximum length, emojis, hashtag count]
Read any text in the image, combine it with my description, and write a ready-to-copy post with relevant hashtags.

## Instagram product post

Use $social-image-post for Instagram. The uploaded image shows our new handmade tote bags. Emphasize everyday styling and the patterns visible in the photo. Tone: playful and warm. End with “Which pattern is your favorite?” Include five relevant hashtags. Do not add prices or material claims unless provided.

## Facebook event poster

Use $social-image-post to turn this event poster into a Facebook post. Description: invite local families to our community art workshop. Tone: welcoming and informative. Extract the date, time, venue, and registration details if readable. Include relevant hashtags. Ask me if any essential detail is unclear.

## LinkedIn announcement

Use $social-image-post for LinkedIn using this launch graphic. Description: we are introducing an appointment-booking service for small salons. Tone: professional and optimistic. Highlight the launch without inventing features or customer results. Keep the full post under 900 characters, with two relevant hashtags.

## Nepali caption

Use $social-image-post for Facebook. Description: introduce the new clothing collection shown in the uploaded image and invite customers to message us for details. Tone: friendly and conversational. Write in Nepali, preserve the brand name as shown, and include relevant English hashtags. Do not invent discounts or delivery promises.

## Correct a poster detail

Use $social-image-post for Instagram. This poster has an old price; use NPR 1,500 instead of the printed price. Description: announce our weekend offer. Tone: upbeat, without exaggerated urgency. Preserve other readable offer conditions. Include three relevant hashtags.

## Multiple platforms

Use $social-image-post with this café photo. Description: introduce our new reading corner and invite people to visit. Write separate Instagram and Facebook captions. Tone: cozy and welcoming. Adapt the wording to each platform and include relevant hashtags. No discounts or unsupported menu details.

## Show image text as well

Use $social-image-post for X. First list readable text from the uploaded image and mark anything uncertain. Then write a cheerful post based on the image and this description: “Announcing our new pottery collection.” Keep the complete post, including two relevant hashtags, within 250 characters.
